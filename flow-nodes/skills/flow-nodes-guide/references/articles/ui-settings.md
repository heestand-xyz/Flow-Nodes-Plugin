# Settings

Source route: `/docs/ui/settings`. Media is not bundled; public route availability must be verified.

Settings controls app-wide behavior and appearance. Settings sync through iCloud so your preferences can remain consistent across devices.

## Navigate Settings

Select a section in the sidebar to view its options. On a compact screen, selecting a section opens its detail page.

## Available sections

The sections shown depend on your device and its capabilities.

- **Recommendations** controls assistance such as connection recommendations and suggestions.
- **Cloud** manages iCloud-related behavior.
- **Home** controls how flows and their thumbnail details appear on Home.
- **Canvas** changes Canvas presentation and interaction preferences.
- **Video & Graphics** configures graphics and video behavior.
- **Resolutions** sets preferred graphic resolutions.
- **Time** configures playback and timing behavior.
- **Wires** changes the appearance and behavior of connections.
- **Docs** controls documentation-related behavior.
- **Feedback** provides ways to contact the Flow Nodes team.
- **Optimizations** contains performance-related choices.
- **Accessibility** contains options that make the app easier to see or operate.

**Intelligence** appears on devices that support the required intelligence features.

**MCP** and **External Editor** are available on Mac.

## iCloud sync

When no section is selected, Settings displays **Settings are synced via iCloud.** Synced preferences can take a moment to appear on another device.

Some settings are device-specific because a feature is only available on a particular platform. Those options do not appear on unsupported devices.
